## Comparison with published face-only results

| Face-only system, 8 classes, Luna-Jiménez et al. folds | Accuracy (fold mean) |
|---|---:|
| Luna-Jiménez et al. (2022), OpenFace AUs + bi-LSTM | 62.13 |
| Luna-Jiménez et al. (2022), static MLP | 60.22 |
| This study: MediaPipe blendshape statistics + XGBRF (3 seeds) | 57.9 (pooled 58.1, 95% CI 53.9–62.6) |

On the same five actor folds and the same eight classes, the blendshape model scores 57.9% (fold mean), 4.2 points below the AU + bi-LSTM model (62.13%) and 2.3 points below the static MLP (60.22%). The comparison is indicative only:

- **Different face features.** This study summarises MediaPipe Face Landmarker blendshapes (52 ARKit coefficients + `_neutral`) into 376 clip-level statistics; Luna-Jiménez et al. use OpenFace facial action units.
- **No model selection on held-out actors here.** XGBRF runs with fixed hyperparameters (set in advance, not tuned on these folds) and no early stopping, so in these runs the held-out actors are used only for testing.
- **Seeds.** Our numbers are averaged over three seeds, with an actor-bootstrap CI for the pooled accuracy.
